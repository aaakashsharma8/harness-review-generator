# Principal Engineer (Principal) - Career Framework

**Scope:** Company-wide or major business unit  
**Focus:** Vision, leverage, long-term technical strategy

### Technical Excellence
- Defines long-term technical vision aligned with company strategy
- Solves ambiguous, high-impact technical problems with no obvious solution
- Shapes architectural direction across multiple domains
- Identifies inflection points and steers the organization through them
- Sets standards for technical excellence across the company

### Execution & Delivery
- Owns outcomes for mission-critical systems or platforms
- Drives execution of large, cross-org technical initiatives
- Balances innovation, stability, and delivery at scale
- Ensures technical strategy translates into real-world outcomes
- Anticipates future technical and organizational challenges

### Collaboration & Influence
- Influences company-wide technical decisions without authority
- Partners closely with senior engineering and product leadership
- Mentors Staff and Principal engineers
- Acts as a stabilizing force during periods of change
- Creates alignment across highly diverse teams and viewpoints

### Customer & Business Impact
- Enables major business bets through technology
- Connects long-term technical investments to company growth
- Improves resilience, scalability, and velocity of core platforms
