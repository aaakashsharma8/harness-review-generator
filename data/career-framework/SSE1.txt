# Senior Software Engineer 1 (SSE1) - Career Framework

### Frontend

#### Technical Excellence
- Designs and builds microfrontends and shared UI foundations
- Implements advanced React patterns and performance optimizations
- Makes sound architectural and technical trade-offs

#### Execution & Delivery
- Delivers complex UI features end-to-end independently
- Breaks down large initiatives into incremental deliverables
- Handles production UI issues effectively

#### Collaboration & Influence
- Mentors junior engineers
- Drives frontend design discussions and documents decisions

#### Customer & Business Impact
- Improves user experience and reduces configuration friction
- Aligns UI work with product and business priorities

---

### Backend

#### Technical Excellence
- Designs and builds backend services from scratch
- Tunes services for performance, scalability, and reliability
- Solves complex production issues by identifying root causes

#### Execution & Delivery
- Owns backend services through deployment and on-call
- Delivers features with minimal handholding

#### Collaboration & Influence
- Leads backend design discussions
- Improves shared backend infrastructure

#### Customer & Business Impact
- Builds scalable backend systems that support business growth
