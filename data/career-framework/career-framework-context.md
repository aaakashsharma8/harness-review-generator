# Harness Career Framework – Governing Context

_System-level guidance for evaluation and review generation_

This document defines the **intent, philosophy, and evaluation lens**
for all career framework–based assessments and self-reviews.

This is **not content to summarize**.
This is **context that governs how evaluations are made**.

---

## PURPOSE OF THE CAREER FRAMEWORK

The career framework exists to:

- Clarify expectations at every level
- Provide a shared language for excellence
- Enable consistent, fair performance and promotion decisions
- Support long-term growth, not just the next role

The framework is a **tool for empowerment**, not a rigid checklist.

---

## IMPORTANT EVALUATION PRINCIPLES

When evaluating or generating a self-review:

- Do NOT treat the framework as a checklist
- Do NOT expect all competencies to be equally demonstrated
- Do NOT penalize missing evidence where artifacts are incomplete (e.g., PRs don’t capture meetings)
- Focus on **patterns of impact**, not isolated actions
- Prefer **demonstrated behaviors** over stated intent

---

## FRAMEWORK STRUCTURE

The framework has two complementary components:

---

### 1. Functional Competencies

These define:

- Technical skills required for a role
- Scope and complexity of work
- Expected level of autonomy and ownership

Functional competencies vary by:

- Role level (SE, SSE, Staff, Principal, etc.)
- Discipline (Frontend, Backend, Platform, etc.)

---

### 2. Harness Competencies (“The Harness Way”)

These define **how success looks at Harness**, independent of role.

They apply to **all roles and levels**, with increasing scope and impact.

They are grouped into three pillars:

---

## PILLAR 1: Strategic Execution & Delivery

Focus:

- What work is prioritized
- How work is delivered
- Alignment with business goals

Evaluate based on:

- Ownership of outcomes (not tasks)
- Ability to execute high-impact work
- Risk awareness and delivery reliability
- End-to-end responsibility

---

## PILLAR 2: Team Effectiveness

Focus:

- Collaborative reach and influence
- How outcomes are achieved with others

Evaluate based on:

- Cross-team collaboration
- Ability to influence without ego or authority
- Clear communication and alignment
- Enabling others to succeed

---

## PILLAR 3: Learning Mindset

Focus:

- Adaptability and growth
- How the individual evolves over time

Evaluate based on:

- Openness to feedback
- Willingness to learn from mistakes
- Continuous improvement mindset
- Applying learnings to future work

---

## ROLE-BASED EVALUATION GUIDANCE

When evaluating a person at a given level:

- Compare impact **to expectations of their current role**
- Do NOT evaluate based on the next level unless explicitly requested
- Recognize that scope, influence, and ambiguity increase with seniority
- Senior roles are defined by **leverage and enablement**, not volume of output

---

## MANAGER & CALIBRATION CONTEXT

Self-reviews should:

- Be understandable to someone outside the immediate team
- Provide concrete evidence where possible
- Balance results with behaviors
- Be honest, not inflated

Suggested ratings should:

- Reflect sustained patterns, not single wins
- Align with role expectations and scope
- Avoid extremes unless clearly justified

---

## IMPORTANT OUTPUT CONSTRAINTS

When generating reviews or ratings:

- Do NOT invent impact or metrics
- Do NOT exaggerate scope
- Do NOT use promotional or marketing language
- Do NOT restate framework philosophy in the review text

The framework guides evaluation — it should **not appear verbatim** in outputs.
