# Architect (Architect) - Career Framework

**Scope:** Enterprise systems and cross-domain architecture  
**Focus:** Coherence, correctness, long-term sustainability

### Technical Excellence
- Designs and governs enterprise-wide architecture
- Ensures consistency, interoperability, and correctness across systems
- Evaluates and evolves core technology choices
- Defines reference architectures and architectural principles
- Anticipates long-term scalability and integration challenges

### Execution & Delivery
- Guides teams in implementing architectural standards
- Reviews and influences designs across multiple domains
- Balances local optimization with global coherence
- Manages architectural risk across the organization

### Collaboration & Influence
- Works closely with engineering, product, and leadership
- Aligns teams around shared architectural direction
- Educates teams on architectural principles and trade-offs
- Acts as final authority on architectural decisions when required

### Customer & Business Impact
- Enables faster, safer delivery through architectural clarity
- Reduces long-term operational and integration costs
- Supports business scalability through sound system design
