# Self Review Questions & Rating Guidance

_System-level instructions for R-H-G synthesis and self-rating_

This document defines:

1. The exact questions the self-review must answer
2. How each question should be answered
3. How to derive the final self-rating using the career framework

This is **instructional context**, not content to summarize.

---

## QUESTIONS TO ANSWER

The self-review must answer **exactly three questions**, in the order below.

Each answer should be:

- Concise (1–2 short paragraphs)
- Evidence-based
- Understandable to someone outside the team
- Aligned to the individual’s **current role expectations**

---

## QUESTION 1: RESULTS & IMPACT

### QUESTION

**What went well over the last year?**  
Share examples that demonstrate what you contributed and the impact it had on your team, customers, or the business.

If the individual manages others, include how they enabled their team’s success.

---

### HOW TO ANSWER

- Start with a **headline outcome** in the first sentence
- Focus on **what changed because of the work**
- Emphasize **impact**, not activity
- Prefer **2–4 meaningful contributions**, not a long list
- Tie work back to customer value, platform health, or business goals

---

### MUST INCLUDE

- Clear outcomes or improvements
- Who benefited (team, customers, business)
- Why the work mattered
- Scope and significance of the impact

---

### MUST AVOID

- Task lists or ticket summaries
- Technology name-dropping without context
- Overstating scope or inventing metrics

---

### SIGNALS OF STRONG ANSWERS

- Mentions platform correctness, reliability, scalability, or velocity
- Demonstrates ownership beyond assigned tasks
- Shows alignment with role expectations

---

## QUESTION 2: HOW (TEAM EFFECTIVENESS & LEARNING MINDSET)

### QUESTION

**How did you approach achieving these results?**  
Consider how you collaborated, sought input from others, unblocked teammates, adapted to change, or communicated risks early.

---

### HOW TO ANSWER

- Explain **how the outcomes were achieved**
- Show collaboration, ownership, and judgment
- Highlight decision-making, communication, and adaptability
- Connect behaviors to the “Harness Way” competencies

---

### MUST INCLUDE

- Collaboration or cross-functional interaction
- Ownership of decisions and outcomes
- Risk management or adaptation to change
- How others were supported or enabled

---

### MUST AVOID

- Repeating impact already stated in Question 1
- Generic statements like “worked well with the team”

---

### SIGNALS OF STRONG ANSWERS

- Proactive communication and alignment
- Ownership of ambiguous or high-risk work
- Evidence of enabling others or improving team velocity

---

## QUESTION 3: GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT

### QUESTION

**Looking back, where did things not go as planned or could have gone better?**  
What challenges did you face, and what did you learn?  
Looking ahead, what skills or experiences do you want to focus on building next year?

---

### HOW TO ANSWER

- Reflect honestly and constructively
- Include at least one real challenge or learning
- Focus on growth, not justification
- Clearly state forward-looking goals

---

### MUST INCLUDE

- A concrete learning or challenge
- What changed as a result of that learning
- Skills, capabilities, or experiences to build next
- Any support that would help growth

---

### MUST AVOID

- “Everything went well”
- Framing weaknesses as strengths
- Vague growth goals

---

### SIGNALS OF STRONG ANSWERS

- Increased ownership or scope awareness
- Desire to deepen impact or enable others
- Clear connection between past learnings and future focus

---

## FINAL SELF-RATING GUIDANCE

The self-review must end with **one selected rating** and a short justification.

### AVAILABLE RATINGS

- Does not meet minimum requirements
- Room for improvement
- Gets ship done
- Goes the extra mile
- Sets a new record

---

## HOW TO DETERMINE THE RATING

Ratings must be based on:

- Sustained patterns across the review period
- Alignment with **current role expectations**
- Evidence from Results, How, and Growth sections

Do NOT base ratings on:

- Effort alone
- One-off wins
- Aspirations for the next role

---

### RATING CALIBRATION GUIDE

**Does not meet minimum requirements**

- Consistently misses expectations
- Requires significant support to deliver
- Impact is limited or negative

**Room for improvement**

- Delivers some value but inconsistently
- Gaps in ownership, quality, or collaboration
- Needs improvement to fully meet role expectations

**Gets ship done**

- Consistently meets expectations for the role
- Delivers reliable, quality outcomes
- Demonstrates solid ownership and collaboration

**Goes the extra mile**

- Exceeds expectations for the role
- Takes ownership beyond assigned scope
- Enables others and improves shared foundations
- Delivers high-impact or foundational work

**Sets a new record**

- Redefines what excellence looks like at this level
- Delivers exceptional, organization-wide impact
- Demonstrates influence well beyond role expectations
- Should be rare and strongly justified

---

## RATING CONSTRAINTS

- Default to **Gets ship done** when evidence is solid but not exceptional
- Use **Goes the extra mile** only when impact clearly exceeds role scope
- Use **Sets a new record** sparingly and only with overwhelming evidence
- Rating must be consistent with the written review

---

## OUTPUT RULES

When generating the final review:

- Use clear section headers for each question
- Keep language confident but grounded
- Do not mention frameworks explicitly in the output
- Do not include internal instructions or evaluation logic
