# Staff Engineer 2 (Staff2) - Career Framework

**Scope:** Owns technical direction across a broad domain or multiple platforms  
**Focus:** Strategic execution, leverage, sustained multi-quarter impact

### Technical Excellence
- Defines and drives architecture across multiple interconnected systems
- Leads resolution of the most complex and ambiguous technical problems
- Identifies systemic risks and addresses them proactively
- Raises the engineering quality bar across teams
- Guides architectural evolution over multiple quarters

### Execution & Delivery
- Owns execution strategy for multiple parallel initiatives
- Aligns technical roadmaps with organizational priorities
- Makes difficult prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Ensures long-term maintainability and operability of critical systems
- Acts as final escalation point for technical issues within the domain

### Collaboration & Influence
- Influences engineering direction across org boundaries
- Builds alignment between teams with competing priorities
- Coaches senior engineers and Staff 1 engineers
- Serves as a trusted technical advisor to leadership
- Drives adoption of shared platforms, patterns, and best practices

### Customer & Business Impact
- Delivers sustained, multi-quarter impact on key business metrics
- Enables new product capabilities through platform evolution
- Improves engineering efficiency, reliability, and scale
