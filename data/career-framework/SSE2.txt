# Senior Software Engineer 2 (SSE2) - Career Framework

### Frontend

#### Technical Excellence
- Leads architecture for complex UI domains
- Drives frontend performance, accessibility, and consistency
- Sets the quality bar for frontend code

#### Execution & Delivery
- Leads delivery across multiple UI workstreams
- Owns reliability of critical frontend surfaces

#### Collaboration & Influence
- Influences technical decisions beyond the immediate team
- Enables other frontend engineers through guidance and reviews

#### Customer & Business Impact
- Drives measurable improvements in user experience

---

### Backend

#### Technical Excellence
- Owns architecture of complex backend domains
- Anticipates and mitigates scalability and reliability risks

#### Execution & Delivery
- Leads delivery of backend initiatives spanning teams
- Owns production health of critical services

#### Collaboration & Influence
- Acts as a backend technical leader across teams
- Mentors senior engineers

#### Customer & Business Impact
- Delivers backend improvements with measurable business impact
