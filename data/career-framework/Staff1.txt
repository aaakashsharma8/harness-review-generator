# Staff Engineer 1 (Staff1) - Career Framework

**Scope:** Owns major technical areas spanning multiple teams  
**Focus:** Enablement, cross-team execution, system correctness

### Technical Excellence
- Designs and evolves large subsystems or platforms used by multiple teams
- Owns system-level architecture within a defined domain
- Anticipates scalability, reliability, and operability concerns early
- Establishes and reinforces technical standards and best practices
- Makes high-quality trade-offs balancing long-term architecture and near-term delivery

### Execution & Delivery
- Leads complex initiatives that span teams or services
- Breaks ambiguous problems into executable plans
- Owns delivery outcomes, not just designs
- Proactively manages technical and delivery risk
- Ensures production readiness across dependent systems

### Collaboration & Influence
- Influences technical direction across teams without formal authority
- Aligns engineers, PMs, and stakeholders around shared technical decisions
- Acts as a technical multiplier by enabling other senior engineers
- Mentors SSEs and emerging Staff engineers
- Drives clarity in complex or contentious technical discussions

### Customer & Business Impact
- Connects technical decisions to customer experience and business outcomes
- Improves platform reliability, scalability, or velocity in measurable ways
- Balances product delivery with reduction of systemic technical debt
