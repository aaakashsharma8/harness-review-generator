# Distinguished Engineer (Distinguished) - Career Framework

**Scope:** Company-wide, long-horizon  
**Focus:** Enduring impact, technical leadership, legacy

### Technical Excellence
- Sets technical direction that shapes the company’s future
- Solves problems others consider unsolvable
- Advances the state of engineering practice within the organization
- Influences technology choices years into the future
- Represents the highest bar of technical excellence

### Execution & Delivery
- Drives transformational technical initiatives
- Guides the organization through major technical shifts
- Ensures sustained excellence across generations of systems
- Balances innovation with operational stability at extreme scale

### Collaboration & Influence
- Influences across the entire company and externally
- Mentors Principals, Staff engineers, and engineering leaders
- Acts as a cultural and technical role model
- Shapes the engineering identity and values of the organization

### Customer & Business Impact
- Enables step-function improvements in company capabilities
- Aligns technology vision with long-term business strategy
- Leaves a lasting technical and organizational legacy
